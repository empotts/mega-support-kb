How each report is placed

  • One report per id. The ticket export comes in overlapping pages, so some rows repeat.
  • By tenant, not by intake’s tag. Intake guesses from the reporter’s browser. The cause depends on the tenant’s locale and booking form (#lib/kb/causes). Every tenant on file writes the day first.
  • hint: a report sent through a booking-v3 form. That form prints MM/DD/YYYY under its date field, people type the month first, and the form reads the day first.
  • parser: every other report from a day-first tenant. The shared parser reads the date month first, so day and month swap (“4 March turned into 3 April”).
  • one-day: the reports the desk read as a one-day move (desk-notes.json). The date is off by one day, not swapped: “Tuesday 6 October showed as Monday”, “Client in Lisbon got Tuesday. Client in Toronto got Monday.” That is a time-zone shift. The desk files these under parser by tenant.

Tickets

Each problem has its own ticket in empotts/mega-support-kb, with the count of reports it covers, the desk’s way. All are closed: #1 fixed, #2 not planned (form retired), #3 a duplicate of #1, because the desk files one-day moves under parser.

Lessons

  • A subject line names a symptom, not a cause. Merging on subject alone hid two bugs inside one ticket.
  • Count by cause, from the data, before you write “known”.
